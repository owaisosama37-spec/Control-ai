---
name: Team sharing boundary
description: Why collaboration shares existing account rooms rather than migrating project ownership
---
Adding collaboration must preserve the existing private-room boundary. Membership grants access to the whole selected room, not just newly created projects. Do not reinterpret first signup, team creation, or membership as authorization to claim unowned legacy data.

**Why:** Existing accounts already own private project histories. Automatically assigning or bulk migrating records would expose unrelated legacy records or silently broaden access.

**How to apply:** Any future legacy import needs an explicit authorized migration with reviewed source ownership. Keep that separate from normal signup and team management. Explain whole-room sharing before granting access.
